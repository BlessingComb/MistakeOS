This draft describes the current product behavior observed in the MistakeOS codebase. It must be reviewed, completed, published at a stable URL, and approved by a qualified professional before public release.

## What we collect

MistakeOS may process account information (email, account ID and chosen display name); learning records you choose to create (such as subjects, notes, exams and recovery activity); optional question photos; explicit private-group content; purchase entitlement information; and limited, privacy-reviewed product events. We do not ask the app to collect passwords, payment card numbers, or device contacts.

## Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, provide learning features, keep your account-backed progress available, enforce service limits, process optional purchases, operate private groups you choose to join, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information or use learning records for targeted advertising in the current product.

## Optional photo analysis

When you request analysis, the selected image is sent through an authenticated MistakeOS server function to the configured AI provider. The current product warns you to remove names, faces, student IDs and other personal information first. Do not submit sensitive information in a photo. Confirm the final AI provider terms, data location, retention and lawful basis before publishing this policy.

## Sharing

Private mistakes and original question photos are not automatically shared with Groups. Information you deliberately post to a private Group is visible to its members. We use service providers needed to operate authentication, cloud data, optional AI analysis, subscriptions and app distribution; the final published policy must name or link to the applicable providers and terms.

## Retention and deletion

You can request deletion from the signed-in account screen. A confirmed deletion removes your Supabase Auth account and associated MistakeOS cloud rows subject to the deployed schema, and clears MistakeOS learning data stored on that device. Subscription cancellation is handled through the relevant app store. The final policy must state backup retention and support request timelines accurately.

## Children and student data

MistakeOS must not be directed to children or school deployments until the owner completes the age, consent and jurisdiction review in `MINORS_DATA_REVIEW.md`. The final public policy must state the applicable age and parent/school process.

## Your choices and contact

You may update local preferences, sign out, manage purchases, and use the account-deletion path. For access, correction, export or support requests, contact `[CONTACT EMAIL]`. The final policy must specify rights and complaint channels applicable to `[COUNTRY/JURISDICTION]`.
